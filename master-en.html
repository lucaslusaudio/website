01

Send Your Material

The process begins with you sending your material and sharing the information about your project. Above all, I want to understand what you are still looking for in your song. It is important that you tell me both what you are already happy with in the mix and what you still feel is missing. The more context I have at this stage, the more productive our email discussion will be.

02

Email Discussion

After reviewing everything you sent, we continue the conversation by email. This step is essential to align my perception of the project with your artistic vision. This exchange ensures that the entire mastering process moves forward with confidence and that we share the same goals before work begins.

03

Mastering

From this point on, every decision follows what we discussed. My goal is simple: to translate the direction we defined together into sound. Unless you specifically want otherwise, my intention is never to leave my signature on your music, but to bring out the very best it already has.

04

First Delivery

You receive the first version and listen carefully at your own pace. I recommend listening for at least one day on as many systems as possible (TV, phone, headphones, car, etc.). Take notes on what worked and what still does not sound as you imagined. Honest feedback is what helps us achieve the result you are looking for.

05

Revisions

Up to three revision rounds are included. Their purpose is to refine the artistic direction we have already established, not to make endless changes. Each round brings the track closer to your vision.

06

Final Delivery

Once the revisions are complete, you receive the final files ready for any platform. If it makes sense for you, we finish with a quick conversation—not to sell anything else, but simply to hear how the experience was from your perspective.
