Privacy Policy

This page explains what data this website collects, what it is used for, and what your rights are. Last updated: September 2026.

What data is collected

Data you provide

When requesting a quote, you provide a name or identifier, an email address or WhatsApp number, and whatever you write about your project.

No field goes beyond what I need in order to reply to you.

Browsing data

The site may record aggregate access data, such as pages visited and traffic source, without identifying you individually.

Audio players are embedded from Spotify, which applies its own cookie policies.

How the data is used

Purpose

Contact data is used solely to respond to your request and to run the project, if it goes ahead.

Sharing

Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for commercial purposes.

Your audio material is never shared publicly without your permission.

Retention

Messages are kept for as long as they are needed for support and project history.

You can request deletion at any time.

Your rights

What you can request

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD), you may request at any time:

  • Confirmation that your data is being processed
  • Access to the data held about you
  • Correction of incomplete or outdated data
  • Deletion of your data
  • Withdrawal of consent

How to exercise them

Just write to studio@lucaslusaudio.com telling me what you need.

I usually reply within the same business day.

Who is responsible

The controller of the data processed on this site is Lucas Lus, an individual working professionally under the name Lucas Lus Audio.
