01

File Submission

The process begins with your file submission. Along with the multitracks and the rough mix (demo), you also send references, information about the song, and your expectations for the final result. This is also the time to tell me what you already love about the track and, more importantly, what still doesn’t sound the way you envision it. The more context I have at this stage, the more productive our email discussion will be.

02

Email Discussion

After reviewing everything you sent, we continue the conversation by email. This step is essential to align my perception of the project and the references you shared with your artistic vision. If I identify anything that requires deeper correction—such as recording, editing, or other technical issues that could affect the final result—I’ll explain what needs to be addressed before mixing begins. This exchange ensures we share the same goals before moving forward.

03

Mixing

From this point on, every decision follows what we agreed on. My goal is simple: to translate the direction we defined together into sound. Unless you specifically want otherwise, my intention is never to leave my signature on your music, but to bring out the very best that is already there.

04

First Delivery

You receive the first version and take your time listening to it. I recommend spending at least a day checking it on as many playback systems as possible (TV, phone, headphones, car, etc.). Take notes on what worked and what still doesn’t sound as you imagined. Feedback is an essential part of the process and helps us reach your vision.

05

Revisions

Up to three revision rounds are included. The limit exists to refine the artistic direction we have already established, not to make endless changes. Each revision brings the track closer to your vision.

06

Final Delivery

Once the revisions are complete, you receive the final files ready for any platform. If you'd like, we can wrap up with a final email exchange—not to sell you anything else, but simply to hear how the experience was from your perspective.
